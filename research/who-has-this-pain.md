## What I could not find

- **Reddit and Hacker News access was blocked at the tool level.** Every direct fetch attempt against reddit.com, old.reddit.com, and one Hacker News item returned errors (WebFetch refused reddit.com domains outright; the HN fetch hit an HTTP 429). The web-search tool's summarized results referenced Reddit threads (e.g., r/legaladvice, a landlord/tenant story via Bored Panda citing Reddit user "karer3is") but did not surface fetchable, quotable verbatim text with working attribution back to the original post — so per the hard guardrail (drop anything without a verifiable source URL/quote), I excluded those.
- **No Twitter/X threads were captured.** I did not find a reliable way to pull verbatim, dated tweets within the search/fetch tools available in this session, so this platform is unrepresented in the findings despite being explicitly requested.
- **No direct hits for the exact "lease clause" / tenant pain point with a verbatim quote.** Multiple searches (r/tenant-style queries, "lease clause surprised didn't read") returned only secondary commentary/listicle sources without quotable, sourced text from an actual tenant.
- **No SaaS Terms of Service horror story with a verbatim quote** (e.g., a review specifically calling out an IP-assignment or liability clause in a SaaS ToS) was found within the search budget — the closest analogues found were subscription/auto-renewal clauses (a related but not identical pain point) and freelance non-compete/IP clauses.
- **Demographics** (age, profession, geography) of the people quoted are mostly unconfirmable beyond what's in the review/complaint itself — Trustpilot and BBB reviewers use partial names/handles with no verified background.
- Given the volume of very similar auto-renewal/subscription complaints found on Trustpilot for a single company (FIXD), I stopped pulling further quotes from that one source once distinct-finding count was met, per the "more is not better" instruction — there is likely a much larger pool of similar consumer subscription-clause complaints across other companies' Trustpilot pages that went unexplored.
