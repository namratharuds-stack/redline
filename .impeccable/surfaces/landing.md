# Landing page surface brief

## Scope and mode

Public route "/", logged-out visitors only (a logged-in visitor is routed past this to the authenticated app home page). Mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action

The reader named in PRD.md: a freelancer, independent contractor, or small-business owner about to accept a document they cannot negotiate from a position of strength (a vendor, contractor, lease, or partnership agreement). Job: decide whether this document is safe to sign. One offered action: try it on a document (routes to sign-up, then upload; no analysis runs without an account, per the earlier no-free-trial decision).

## Proof and content

Demonstrates exactly one thing: a contract turning into ranked flags, each showing the exact sentence it came from. Uses the real product name. No invented prices, customers, or quotes. No verdict on whether to sign, no legal advice claimed, no scanned/photographed documents, no document types outside contract/lease/freelance agreement/ToS.

## Constraints

No pricing or payment messaging anywhere. No testimonials, user counts, or unverifiable statistics; every claim traces to the product's actual mechanism, documented feature list, or PRD.md's cited research.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Redline gives every risky clause the same plastic page-flag a closing agent sticks on the spot where you need to initial: refusing the clean SaaS dashboard-with-checkmarks this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Bond-paper ivory ground (#f4efe6), graphite ink for body text (#2b2b28). Severity spectrum rationed strictly to the physical flag tabs themselves, never to backgrounds or buttons: flag-yellow #e8c93a (low), flag-orange #e2793a (medium), flag-red #c23b2e (high). A confident plain sans for reading copy; a stamped/stencil-style caps face for flag-tab labels and case-file metadata (mirrors real adhesive flags). Components: physical flag tabs (die-cut corner, jutting off the document's right edge, numbered, each holding one short finding), the document rendered as literal bond paper (faint ruled lines, a soft deckle edge), a flag-index block styled as a paper strip (flag color chip, one-line finding, quoted source fragment per row), dotted leader lines connecting a flag tab to its exact quoted sentence in the document body.

STORY: The visitor recognizes the flag-tab ritual from a closing or contract signing they've lived through. They understand Redline marks every risky clause the way a professional would flag a page for them, quoting the exact sentence so they can verify it themselves, then hands them a counter-offer for each flag. They believe this is careful, physical-grade markup, not a chatbot's guess. They act: try it on their own document.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A single contract page in bond-paper grammar occupies the right ~55-60%, three colored flag tabs (yellow, orange, red) jutting from its right edge at realistic clause heights, each holding a two-to-four-word finding label. The left ~40-45% carries a kicker, a one-sentence headline naming the mechanism, the primary CTA ("Try it on your document"), and the flag index's first row (color chip, finding, quoted fragment) already legible without scrolling.

FORM: Sign-Here Flags (Impeccable's Pick; adhesive page-flag tabs), chosen over the assigned "Inspection Findings" direction and the category-standard canon on the decision page. Seed key 48161e5b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
